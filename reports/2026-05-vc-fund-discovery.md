## Mother Ventures Fund I

- **Fund event:** Closed a $10 million debut early-stage fund on May 8, 2026.
- **Thesis:** Mother Ventures is focused on companies where mothers are the
  primary consumer, citing 85 million US mothers who influence 85% of household
  purchases and more than $2.4 trillion in annual purchasing power.
- **Why it is new this month:** The Fund I close was announced in May 2026.
- **Recently disclosed investments:**
  - **Coral Care** - connects families with in-home pediatric developmental
    specialists.
  - **Tin Can** - a modern landline for kids.
  - **Sunfish** - fertility financing and support for IVF access.
  - **FamilyWell** - mental health platform serving women across life stages.
- **Sources:** [Business Wire announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260507109043/en/Mother-Ventures-Closes-%2410-Million-Debut-Fund-to-Back-Companies-Serving-the-%242.4-Trillion-Mom-Market),
  [TechCrunch coverage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/08/mother-ventures-is-looking-at-moms-as-the-economic-engine/).

## Kalos Ventures Inaugural Fund

- **Fund event:** Announced an oversubscribed $78.8 million inaugural fund on
  May 11, 2026.
- **Thesis:** Kalos leads early-stage rounds for technology companies addressing
  workforce, care, and education needs. Its thesis centers on two shifts: AI
  reshaping a large share of US jobs and aging demographics creating new care
  infrastructure needs.
- **Why it is new this month:** The inaugural fund announcement was released in
  May 2026.
- **Recently disclosed investments / current portfolio examples:**
  - **Manifest** - AI-powered platform for small business owners.
  - **Rosarium Health** - home safety assessments and accessibility
    modifications for aging in place.
  - **Learn In** - deep skilling, skills academies, and education benefits;
    acquired by Degreed.
  - **Transfr** - classroom-to-career pathway and VR-based workforce training.
  - **Tilt** - software for leave management and employer compliance.
- **Sources:** [Business Wire announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260511930435/en/Kalos-Ventures-Announces-Oversubscribed-%2478.8M-Inaugural-Fund-to-Back-Early-Stage-Companies-Advancing-Workforce-Care-and-Education),
  [Kalos portfolio page](https://www.kalosventures.com/).

## AlphaDrive Fund I

- **Fund event:** Launched a $100 million fund on May 5, 2026.
- **Thesis:** AlphaDrive backs companies at the intersection of AI and
  cybersecurity, including AI-native products that automate workflows, replace
  manual security labor with agents, and use venture/private-equity style
  company-building or roll-up strategies.
- **Why it is new this month:** The fund launch was announced in May 2026.
- **Recently disclosed investments:**
  - **Beacon**
  - **Inspectio**
  - **Shield8**
  - **Predict**
  - **Stealth startup** - disclosed as stealth; no public company name provided.
- **Sources:** [PR Newswire announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/yaron-elad-and-elik-etzion-launch-alphadrive-a-100-million-fund-focusing-on-cyber-and-ai-investments-302762611.html),
  [VCWire portfolio disclosure](https://vcwire.tech/2026/05/06/alphadrive-ventures-launches-first-100m-fund/).

## A* Fund III

- **Fund event:** Announced a $450 million third fund on May 12, 2026.
- **Thesis:** A* describes itself as sector-generalist but stage-specialist,
  backing exceptional builders at seed before consensus, traction, or sometimes
  even product. The firm concentrates capital and time around a smaller number of
  founders rather than organizing around a single market thesis.
- **Why it is new this month:** Fund III was announced in May 2026.
- **Recently disclosed investments / current portfolio examples:**
  - **Ramp** - finance automation platform.
  - **Decagon** - conversational AI platform for AI agents.
  - **Whop** - ecommerce platform for digital commerce.
  - **Cape** - private, secure communications over commercial cellular networks.
  - **Mercor** - AI global labor marketplace.
- **Sources:** [A* Fund III announcement](https://fund.a-star.co/),
  [TechCrunch coverage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/12/kevin-hartzs-a-just-closed-its-third-fund-with-450-million/),
  [A* companies page](https://a-star.co/companies).
