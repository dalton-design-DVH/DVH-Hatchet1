# Monthly VC Fund Discovery Workflow

Use this workflow to find newly announced or newly closed VC funds each month and
summarize their thesis plus 3-5 recently disclosed investments.

## Inclusion criteria

Include a fund when all of the following are true:

1. The fund was announced, launched, or closed during the report month.
2. The strategy is venture capital, seed, early-stage, growth venture, or a
   clearly adjacent startup investment strategy.
3. Public sources disclose a credible investment thesis.
4. Public sources disclose at least three portfolio investments, recently
   disclosed investments, or current portfolio examples tied to the firm.

Put funds that meet criteria 1-3 but not criterion 4 in a watchlist section
instead of forcing weak portfolio evidence.

## Source priority

Prefer sources in this order:

1. Fund or firm announcement pages.
2. Press releases from Business Wire, PR Newswire, GlobeNewswire, or equivalent.
3. Portfolio pages on the fund's official website.
4. Reputable venture/startup publications that link back to primary material.
5. Databases or aggregators only as secondary confirmation.

## Suggested search queries

Run these with the target month and year filled in:

- `"new venture fund" "[Month] [Year]" thesis portfolio`
- `"Fund I" venture capital "[Month] [Year]" investments`
- `"closes" "venture fund" "[Month] [Year]" "portfolio"`
- `site:businesswire.com venture capital fund closes [Month] [Year] portfolio`
- `site:prnewswire.com launches fund venture capital [Month] [Year] AI cyber`
- `site:globenewswire.com "fund" "venture" "[Month] [Year]"`
- `site:techcrunch.com venture fund [Month] [Year]`

## Report format

Each monthly report should include:

- Scope and as-of date.
- One summary table of included funds.
- One subsection per fund with:
  - fund size and launch or close date,
  - thesis in 1-3 bullets,
  - 3-5 named investments,
  - source links.
- A watchlist section for relevant funds without enough public investment data.
- A source log with primary URLs.

## Quality checks

- Do not infer an investment thesis from marketing copy alone when a primary
  source states one directly.
- Distinguish "new firm" from "new fund by an existing firm."
- Label stealth investments as stealth instead of inventing names.
- If a portfolio company appears on a general firm page but not in the fund
  announcement, phrase it as a "current portfolio example" unless the source ties
  it specifically to the new fund.
